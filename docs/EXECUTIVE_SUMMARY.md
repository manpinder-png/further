# Discover · Learn · Connect
## Executive summary | Meta AI hackathon concept

> **One sentence:** An AI-native learning platform that turns curiosity into personalized, measurable learning journeys and brings people together when collaboration improves understanding.

### The opportunity
YouTube and TikTok make information easy to discover; courses provide depth but often require choosing a fixed path. General AI chat can explain almost anything, but it rarely provides a cohesive, persistent, socially connected learning experience out of the box. **We connect discovery, adaptive practice, and purposeful peer learning in one product.**

### The product in 30 seconds

```text
             DISCOVER                  LEARN                    CONNECT
       What sparks curiosity?    What helps me understand?   Who helps me grow?
                |                         |                         |
      Search or curiosity feed --> Adaptive learning moment --> Optional peer session
                ^                         |                         |
                +---- Next curiosity <-- Evidence of progress <-- Shared reflection
```

**Discover:** Start with anything, even just a topic name. A short curiosity feed can suggest related ideas, but never requires endless scrolling.

**Learn:** AI establishes a starting point, builds a *living* concept path, selects an appropriate explanation or activity, and checks understanding. A user can explore for two minutes or pursue calculus for months. There is no mandatory prewritten course, but prerequisite concepts and verified material anchor serious learning.

**Connect:** AI recommends a partner or small group when their complementary knowledge, goals, availability, and preferences make collaboration useful. A shared challenge, simulation, roleplay, or guided discussion gives the group a purpose. Solo learning always works.

### The magic moment
A person types **“Calculus”** without knowing where to begin. The app discovers they need a refresher on functions, demonstrates change through an interactive graph, asks them to predict a result, adjusts the explanation, then suggests either the next concept or a five-minute collaborative challenge. The person leaves able to explain something they couldn't explain before.

### Product principles
- **Curiosity first, mastery real:** Make discovery inviting; measure independent understanding rather than minutes watched.
- **Adaptive, not arbitrary:** Personalize sequence and presentation, while respecting prerequisite knowledge.
- **Social with a purpose:** Match on *specific concepts and collaborative benefit*, not just broad topics.
- **Multiple formats, one engine:** Micro-explanations, visualizations, puzzles, practice, roleplay, projects, and discussions are reusable activity templates.
- **No forced multiplayer:** Learning never depends on a crowded network.
- **Responsive by design:** Same core experience on mobile and desktop; accessible touch and keyboard controls.
- **Respectful engagement:** Encourage return through meaningful progress, not manipulative infinite-scroll mechanics.

### How it differs
| Alternative | Typical strength | Our added value |
|---|---|---|
| Video/social feeds | Easy discovery | Interactive understanding checks and persistent learning paths |
| Fixed courses | Structured depth | Adaptive starting points, branches, and pace |
| General AI chat | On-demand explanation | Shared learner model, activity engine, progress evidence, and peer matching |
| Study-group apps | Finding people | AI creates the *reason and structure* for learning together |

### Hackathon MVP: prove one complete loop
1. **Discover:** Search for a topic and see a small curated discovery feed.
2. **Calibrate:** Answer 1–3 low-friction questions to establish a tentative starting point.
3. **Learn:** Complete an AI-personalized learning moment using two robust templates: guided explanation + interactive challenge.
4. **Connect:** Match two or three browser sessions around a compatible concept; run a shared text-based challenge with AI facilitation.
5. **Reflect:** Independently explain or apply the concept; record progress and suggest the next step.

**Demo scenario:** Three learners enter different prompts about GPUs and parallel processing. The system maps them to related concepts, proposes a useful shared challenge, and each learner leaves with an updated concept profile.

**Deliberately not in MVP:** Video calling, unrestricted generated code execution, universal subject mastery, full multilingual voice, sophisticated public reputation scoring, a production-scale feed, and support for minors.

### Success signals
- Time from first prompt to first useful learning interaction.
- Before/after performance on a novel explanation or application question.
- Percentage of recommended matches that produce a completed useful activity.
- User-rated usefulness and willingness to return; later, delayed retention tests.

### Startup trajectory
**Phase 1:** Prove learning value in a narrow adult technical community. **Phase 2:** Expand reusable concept maps, activity templates, and collaborative formats. **Phase 3:** Add deeper journeys, recommendations, and creator/educator partnerships after validating quality, economics, and safety.

### Pitch
**Discover something worth knowing. Learn it your way. Connect when learning together makes it better.**
